# JR Bar Community Code of Conduct

JR Bar is a fork of SidePulse. This project welcomes contributions that make
the macOS application, its providers, hardware integrations, documentation,
and release process more useful and more trustworthy.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful, direct, and constructive;
- discuss the work and its evidence rather than speculate about a person;
- provide reproducible details for bugs and compatibility reports;
- protect private information, credentials, prompts, transcripts, paths, and
  account data in issues, pull requests, fixtures, and logs;
- disclose security or privacy vulnerabilities privately, as described in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), rather than in a public issue;
- follow the repository's contribution, testing, and release boundaries in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted
contact, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publication of someone
else's private information are not acceptable. Do not submit malicious code,
real secrets, real user content, or fixtures copied from private sessions.

Impersonation, social engineering, and attempts to bypass consent, credential,
permission, signing, or hardware-safety boundaries are also out of scope for
this community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, close, or remove material and may restrict
participation when behavior conflicts with this policy or puts users, their
data, or the project at risk. Decisions are based on the available evidence
and the safety of the project. There is no promise of a particular enforcement
step or response time.

For a conduct concern that should not be public, contact the maintainer at
`Contact@JonathanRReed.com`. Do not use a public issue for a suspected
security or privacy vulnerability; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

This document applies in the repository, its issue and review spaces, and
project-related interactions where a participant is representing JR Bar.
